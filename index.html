Wind-Up Slider

A giant swinging an arm, on a loop. Wind-up is how many frames the arm pulls back. Lag is how many frames it hangs at the top. Total is fixed, so more wind-up means a faster hit. Drag the sliders and watch where heavy turns into just fast.

Late-hit rule vs broken twins

A hit more than 30 ms off the beat (early or late) is late: it resets the combo and the multiplier. Exactly 30 is on time. Every 5 on-time hits adds 1 to the multiplier. Each twin breaks one part and must fail at least one case.
